### combat.bi.no.clankerslop
<br>
<br>
I liked the concept of the combat.ai plugin by Zuckung, but I didn't like the AI image. So here is a plugin to change them. All images are 100% real, taken and edited by me personally. No 3D models, and NO AI.<br>
<br>
Needless to say you need Zuckung's combat.ai plugin for this to work.<br>
